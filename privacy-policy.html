YOUR PRIVACY MATTERS

Privacy Policy

We respect the personal information and photographs customers provide for personalized products and aim to use them only for the purpose for which they were submitted.

Information We Receive

Depending on your order, we may receive information such as your name, contact details, delivery information, photographs, custom text, product choices and order-related communication.

How Customer Images Are Used

Photographs and other images supplied for customization are used to prepare and print the customer's requested product. They may be accessed by the people or production systems that need the information to complete the order.

Deletion of Customer Images

Customer-provided images are intended for order production and are not intended to be retained indefinitely. Once the product has been printed and the production requirement has been completed, the working image files should be deleted from the production system according to our operational deletion process.

We do not intend to use customer photographs for marketing, public display or social media without appropriate permission.

Contact & Order Information

Basic order and contact information may need to be retained for customer support, order history, accounting, legal or business record requirements. Such information is handled only for legitimate business purposes.

Third-Party Services

Where delivery, payment, messaging or other services are provided by third-party providers, information necessary to complete that service may be shared with the relevant provider. Those providers may have their own privacy policies.

Contact Us

If you have a question about your personal information or the handling of a customer image, please contact us at support@amorastudios.in.

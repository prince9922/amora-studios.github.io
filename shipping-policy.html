CUSTOMER CARE

Shipping Policy

We prepare every personalized order with care and aim to make delivery simple and transparent.

Order Processing

Personalized products require preparation and printing before dispatch. Processing time may vary depending on the product, customization requirements and order volume.

Orders are processed after the required customization details, such as photographs or text, have been received and confirmed.

Delivery

Once your order is dispatched, it is handed over to the applicable delivery partner. Delivery time can vary by destination, courier availability, weather, holidays and other logistics conditions.

For urgent requirements, please contact us before placing the order so we can confirm whether the requested timeline is feasible.

Customized Orders

Because personalized products are created specifically for the customer, please check your photos, names, dates and other customization information carefully before confirming the order.

Delivery Issues

If a package is delayed, damaged or appears to have a delivery issue, contact Amora Studios as soon as possible with your order details so we can assist with the courier or replacement process where applicable.

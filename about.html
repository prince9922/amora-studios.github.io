About Amora Studios

We turn memories into gifts.

Amora Studios is a personalised gifting brand built around one simple idea: a gift becomes more meaningful when it carries something personal — a favourite photo, a name, a message, a special date or a memory.

From everyday keepsakes to thoughtful gifts for birthdays, anniversaries, festivals and special occasions, we create products designed to make moments last a little longer.

Our story

Personalisation is at the heart of what we do.

Amora Studios was created to make personalised gifting simple, accessible and genuinely special. Instead of choosing a generic gift from a shelf, customers can add their own story to it.

A photograph from a favourite day, the name of someone special, a heartfelt message or a design created just for the occasion can become part of the final product.

We focus on personalised products across categories such as mugs, magnets, T-shirts, frames, cushions, keychains and bottles, giving customers multiple ways to turn their ideas into tangible gifts.
